To reach a healthy BMI of 18.5, I need to weigh at least 51.6 kg. My 6-month target of 50 kg gets me very close to that mark.
🩺
Confirmed deficiency
My blood tests show only Vitamin D deficiency. This directly affects my oral health — low Vitamin D is a primary cause of recurring mouth ulcers. It also weakens my immune system and reduces muscle recovery ability.
⚡
Why I look weak
At 40 kg / 5'5", I have very little muscle mass. My body doesn't have reserves. This makes me visibly thin and fatigues quickly. The fix is caloric surplus + resistance training — eating more than my body burns while building muscle.
🫀
Mouth ulcers — root cause
The ulcers are triggered by junk food (MSG, maida, spices) + Vitamin D deficiency. My stomach also reacts because the gut lining is sensitized. As my nutrition and Vitamin D levels improve, ulcer frequency will reduce significantly.

Is 10 kg in 6 months possible?
Yes — very achievable for a 19-year-old. That's ~0.4 kg/week, which is a healthy and realistic rate. The key requirements: consistent caloric surplus daily, resistance training 3×/week, and 8+ hours of sleep every night.

Week 5–6: Add another 2.5 kg

⚠️ Critical gym rules
🍌 Eat within 45 min after every workout — milk + banana minimum. This is the muscle-building window.
🚫 Zero running or heavy cardio — burns 400–600 kcal/session. I need every calorie for gaining.
🍽️ Never train on empty stomach — eat a small meal 45–60 min before gym or performance suffers badly.
😴 Sleep is training too — 80% of muscle repair happens in deep sleep. Less than 7 hours = wasted gym session.

D3: 60,000 IU weekly (as doctor prescribed) · K2 MK-7: 100 mcg daily
Directly linked to my mouth ulcers and immune weakness. D3 reduces ulcer frequency and severity. K2 helps D3 direct calcium to bones (not arteries) — always take them together.

Improves energy levels, appetite, and nerve function. Also reduces mouth ulcer episodes. Contains B12 (vegetarian gap), B6, B9 (folate). Cheap and highly effective — no reason not to take this.

Section 06 — Non-Negotiable Rules

The habits that make or break this.

Diet and gym are the plan. These rules are the execution. Ignoring even two of these will slow progress by 50%.

01
Never skip a meal
Skipping one meal = 400–700 calories lost. This is the single biggest reason underweight people stay underweight. Set phone alarms for meal times if needed.
02
Eat even when not hungry
At 40 kg, my appetite signals are suppressed. My stomach is small. I need to eat on a schedule, not when I feel hungry. Hunger will follow consistent eating within 3–4 weeks.
03
Sleep 8+ hours, every night
Growth hormone is released during deep sleep. 70% of muscle repair happens at night. Bad sleep = wasted diet + gym work. This is as important as eating.
04
Drink water between meals, not before
Drinking water just before eating fills the stomach and reduces food intake. Drink water 30 minutes after meals. Stay hydrated at 2.5L/day total.
05
Add ghee to everything
Ghee is not unhealthy. It's one of the best calorie-dense foods for vegetarians. 1 tsp = 45 kcal. Add to roti, dal, rice, khichdi. It adds up to 200–300 extra kcal daily with minimal effort.
06
15 min morning sunlight — daily
Helps the body produce Vitamin D naturally alongside supplements. Sets the circadian rhythm for better sleep. Improves mood and energy levels. Free and zero effort.
✗
Zero junk food or outside food
Momos, spicy street food, packaged chips — these trigger my mouth ulcers and disrupt my gut. Not just for this month. For the entire 6-month program. No exceptions.
✗
Zero cardio (no running)
Running burns 400–600 kcal per session — calories my body urgently needs for gaining. After reaching 55 kg, I can add light cardio for fitness. Not before.
